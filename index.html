How the sound and study mode work

Study mode starts with a 400 Hz tone in your left ear and a 440 Hz tone in your right ear: a 40 Hz difference. During breaks, the right tone gradually falls to 410 Hz, then returns to 440 Hz before the next focus block. You can also choose the lower 200 Hz pitch or the study’s 320/360 Hz pair (340 Hz centre during 40 Hz focus). With the 320 Hz base, the break uses 320/330 Hz. Only the focus tone pair matches the study; the break schedule is this app’s own design.

Each round includes 25 minutes of focus and a 5-minute break. The first 30 seconds of the break fade from 40 to 10 Hz, the middle four minutes hold at 10 Hz, and the last 30 seconds return to 40 Hz. After your chosen rounds, audio stops. Take a longer break before starting another session. Pause session silences the audio and freezes the timer; Resume session continues from the same point. Stop ends the session; starting again begins round one.

The difference sets the beat rate; the carrier pitch can affect perception and the measured auditory response. 400/440 Hz is an option, not a proven study improvement over 200/240 Hz. The 10 Hz break is a lower-frequency preset, not a guarantee of calm or brain entrainment.

Use stereo headphones and turn off mono audio in your device’s accessibility settings. Spatial audio or other sound processing may change the channel separation. The ear checks help you confirm your setup. Starting an ear check ends the current session.

Keep your device awake for uninterrupted playback. The mode names describe frequency presets, not guaranteed effects. Start at a comfortable, low volume.
